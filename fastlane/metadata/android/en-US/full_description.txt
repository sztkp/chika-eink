Chika-eInk is an independent Android fork of Chika by Chakra (Chalchitra Krida),
initially targeting the BOOX Palma 2. It preserves Chika's comic reading functionality
while adapting the UI for e-ink: monochrome controls, high contrast, static indicators,
and immediate panel transitions. BOOX reader refresh integration falls back safely on unsupported devices.

Features:

* Reads CBZ/ZIP archives.
* On-device panel detection — a small neural model finds panels and speech balloons. Fully offline;
  nothing is uploaded.
* Smart grouping — tiny adjacent panels are merged into one comfortable zoom, and oversized panels
  are split with a bubble-aware cut.
* Reader controls — tap to step through panels, swipe to turn pages, pinch and drag to pan, a compact
  Progress panel, a "show whole page" button, and a left-to-right / right-to-left (manga) toggle.
* Library — import comics from your device, cover thumbnails, and resume exactly where you left off
  (page and panel). Sort by title, last added, or last read; remove comics or reset
  progress with confirmation. Standard volume/page buttons navigate in the reader.
* No accounts, no ads, no trackers, no network access.
